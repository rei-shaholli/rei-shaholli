# Outline: nine circles

Two lines, braided. The **Mars line** is the present (2125) and climbs. The **Earth line** is the past (2114 to 2124) and descends. Earth chapters follow Dante's circles down. Mars chapters are Purgatory: the climb toward understanding. There is no Paradise. Virgil leaves Peter at the top of the mountain.

Chapters alternate, roughly M / E / M / E. The Earth line ends with Peter boarding the convoy. The Mars line ends with the council and what comes after. They meet on the last page.

The 2013 label "Eighth Circle" on the Alaska chapter is what suggested this. Fraud is the eighth circle. Treachery is the ninth.

---

## Frame
**Prologue: the letter.** Peter writing to the child (D4). Short. "I want to tell you a story... it is my legacy, so listen to it and you might survive." He knows it may never be read. Half a page.

## 1. Limbo (Mars)
Day 31. Ron, the drone, the Muse. Rebuild around the hook: the water module is receiving a signal that rewrites its code at runtime, and the rumor that Earth has gone quiet. End on Brett's whisper: "what if it is It." Chapter 1 must end with "It has reached us," not "Earth is sad."
*Demonstrates:* Peter carrying a truth no one else can see.

## 2. Lust (Earth, 2114)
Not sex. Appetite for comfort. Peter at 18, warm bed, pancakes, the pit in his heart he cannot name, the Mars 1 memory. College as a transaction. Brett, Dan, Christine. Then the sirens, and the class learns of the DC bomb. The world chooses safety over everything, with relief.
*Demonstrates:* a people choosing the cave.

## 3. Mars: the transmissions
Peter asks Liz and Dr. Jiangsu for the Earth logs. He is a cryptanalyst; he assumes cipher. Begins work. Introduce the first of Lilly's old messages, still readable. Set up D5 dimension 1: "I" is already thinning in the newest ones.
*Demonstrates:* the first hairline crack in Mars's assumption that Earth is dead.

## 4. Gluttony (Earth, 2120)
The office. The cubicle grid, Tim, the bathroom timer, the cold water. Dinner with Dan at ChiTown. The fly that is a drone. The military convoy. The table shakes. Society consuming people and calling it efficiency.
*Demonstrates:* the state's appetite; Dan's sincere belief that it is right.

## 5. Mars: the water module
Nudge 5. The signal in the WEM is not random. Peter and Brett trace it. It is not an attack; nothing is damaged. It is a probe, or a greeting, or a reflex. Alex's uranium question lands: without Earth, how long? Liz: the medical telemetry from Earth stopped making sense before the words did.
*Demonstrates:* contact with something that does not share the concept "attack."

## 6. Greed (Earth, 2120)
The Hunt. Sept 14. Taken from bed. The truck. Facility 44, the conveyor belts, the drones like bees. Nudge 1: the man beside him whispers "stay calm." The state hoarding everyone.
*Demonstrates:* the physical channel of what will later become possession.

## 7. Mars: the seat
Nudge 3. Peter learns who was removed from the convoy so he could board. A name, a face, a reason that looks mundane and is not. He tells no one.
*Demonstrates:* the cost of his freedom.

## 8. Wrath (Earth, 2120 to 2121)
Inside Jeff's network. The resistance's win. Peter's "invaluable contribution." Lilly: "why so serious, always?" The anger of people who have figured out they are being played.
*Demonstrates:* the righteous crowd Jason describes, forming.

## 9. Mars: Lilly's messages
Peter works through her messages in order. The drift, one message at a time. Dimension 2: she stops naming things and starts naming what they are doing. The last one he can read. The first one he cannot.
*Demonstrates:* the thesis, as grief.

## 10. Heresy (Earth, 2121)
Jason. The drive with Jeff. The surprise party, "we been waiting for you." The white basement. Jason's first sermon, and the object Peter notices and does not understand. Virgil introduced as a tool. Peter recruited.
*Demonstrates:* the heretic against the state's religion, who has his own.

## 11. Mars: the partition
D7. Peter returns to the memory card. The unopenable partition. He begins to see that its structure matches the drift in the transmissions. He cannot read it. He knows who wrote it.
*Demonstrates:* that he was written to, in advance.

## 12. Violence (Earth, 2121 to 2122)
Building toward Alaska. Jason's lesson on manipulation, told as a story about "a physicist." Peter hears it as theory. The resistance's actions and the state's. Lilly and Peter, a life beginning.
*Demonstrates:* the moment Peter could have understood and chose not to.

## 13. Mars: the council
Peter tells them. The speech, in his voice, undercut. Dan beside him. The three positions laid out without being named. The council decides to stay human and not rebuild Earth.
*Demonstrates:* living with uncertainty, as policy.

## 14. Fraud (Earth, 2122)
Alaska. The cold. The facility. The Sphere: the colorful foggy globe, names on particles, pictures in the fog. Jason behind the terminal. Virgil plugged in. The simulations running on their own. "Virgil is running the simulations. He should be stopping soon." Nudge 2 in hindsight: it was too easy.
*Demonstrates:* the sculpture seen from every angle at once.

## 15. Mars: after the speech
Peter alone. Rereads what he said. Hears Jason. Does not know if that is becoming a preacher or just what conviction sounds like. The water module signal returns, once.
*Demonstrates:* the book refusing to be sure Peter is right.

## 16. Treachery (Earth, 2122 to 2124)
Jason's confession. Plato's cave, inverted. The memory card. Jason's death. Lilly: "I'm pregnant. I can't come." Peter boards the convoy. The ninth circle is ice; the convoy launches into cold.
*Demonstrates:* the man who hated manipulators, as the greatest one.

## Coda: the letter, again
Peter closes the letter to the child. The parting advice from the 2013 notes, cut to its bones: "Do not follow preachers, for they are just as lost." Said by a man who did. The last transmission from Earth, or the sculpture, or silence.
