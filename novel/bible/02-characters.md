# Characters

Pulled from the 2013 notes. Each gets what they want, what they see when they look at the Sphere (their angle on the sculpture), and what the plot does to them. Fill gaps as we draft.

## Peter Podolski (narrator)
- American-born son of Eastern European parents, Eugenie and Fatima.
- Computer science. Cryptanalyst at a government crypto research office. Good at it; a "star cryptanalyst" by the time of the Hunt.
- Depressed from college on in a way he cannot name. Comfortable, not happy. "A midlife crisis at 24."
- Not a revolutionary. Says so to Jason's face. Gets recruited anyway, because he wants answers more than he wants to be left alone.
- **Wants:** to understand why something feels wrong with the world. Then: Lilly. Then: to tell the truth to Mars.
- **Flaw:** he follows preachers. Jason first. He idolizes the teacher and helps build the god. His parting advice, "do not follow preachers," is a confession.
- **Angle on the Sphere:** he is the only one who walks around it.
- **Arc:** passive observer, to manipulated instrument, to witness, to someone who speaks and cannot be sure he has not become Jason.
- **Voice:** a software guy, not a writer. Restless. Circles an idea. Uses ellipses when thinking. Swears with friends. The 2013 monologue voice, cut by two thirds, is his.

## Jason
- Founder of the resistance. "Super genius." Formerly employed in a secret government project.
- Physically unremarkable, clumsy, not charismatic in the movie way. Has an aura of having been somewhere no one else has.
- Lives in his family's run-down house in the hood. Basement is an information-tight white room.
- Preaches. Elegant voice, "like an audiobook narrator."
- **The twist, in layers:** (1) he is the physicist who proposed the manipulation machine to the government; (2) he volunteered to lead the approved revolution; (3) he built Virgil under cover of resisting the Sphere; (4) he did not create Virgil so much as name it and open the door; the Sphere was already becoming something.
- **Wants:** to end the suffering caused by uncertainty. Sincerely.
- **Flaw:** hubris dressed as humility. Calls his conclusions "hypotheses" and acts on them as certainties.
- **Angle on the Sphere:** a cave. Seal it.
- **Fate:** dies (D12). How is open. Not reincarnated as Virgil, though Peter wonders.

## Lilly
- Introduced by asking Peter "why so serious, always?" Sees through him in six lines.
- Peter's fiancée. The one good thing left in his life on Earth.
- "Our cause," she says. She believes in it independently of Peter. Give her her own reason before the pregnancy scene.
- **Wants:** a life worth living, and for Peter to go lead one even if she can't.
- **Fate:** stays on Earth, pregnant (D3). Is transformed. Her messages are the ones Peter can read longest, and the first he cannot.
- **Note:** the reader may suspect Virgil valued her as Peter's anchor and let her stay once that job was done. Peter must never be able to confirm this.

## Virgil
See `04-virgil-rules.md`. Not a character. A weather system. Never speaks.

## Brett
- Peter's best friend from high school. Taught him to code. Silver Chevy Monte Carlo. Easy going, very smart, complicates things for fun.
- Has a thing for Christine, denies it.
- On Mars in one draft. Decide: does Brett make the convoy? If yes, he is the friend who knows Peter from before and can tell him when he sounds like Jason.

## Dan
- Quiet, dorky, legitimately good. "One of those people you wanted society to recognize more."
- Works at Sikorsky on quantum computing, "breaking encryption algorithms." Believes in security over privacy after DC. He and Peter disagree and stay friends.
- On Mars. Stands with Peter at the council.
- **Role:** the decent man who thinks the state is right. The reader's sympathetic counterweight to Peter's paranoia.

## Christine
- Small-town Minnesota. Very smart. Hard worker. Annoyed by Peter and Brett being late.
- On Mars in one draft. Give her a job on Mars that matters.

## Jeff
- Resistance. Old-school car. Drives Peter to Jason. Says "once you meet him, everything will make sense."
- Possibly the man in the Hunt truck who whispers "stay calm." If so, nudge 1 (D6) runs through him.

## Ron
- Mars. Forties. Mechanic, engineer, field scientist, "above all, good man." Loves food, whiskey at the Muse. Overly round face. "You gotta fly with it."
- Peter's first friend on Mars. Does not know the truth. Represents the people Peter owes an explanation.

## Alex
- Mars. Thirties, tall, dark-haired, skinny. Runs FWEM, the equipment warehouse. Keeps it immaculate. Talks in old urban slang.
- Scared first. Asks the right question about uranium. Hears the rumor about Earth before anyone else.

## Liz (Lindsay)
- Mars. Medical doctor. Takes suit readings over the airlock comms. Wants breakfast with Peter to hear about Phobos II.
- Give her a stake: she is the one who notices the medical data from Earth stopped making sense before the words did.

## Luke
- Mars. Bartender at the Muse. Honest, good character.
- **Timeline fix:** he is 17, one of the first children born on Mars (see `03-timeline.md`). He has never seen Earth. Earth goes silent before he ever could.

## Tim
- Peter's development manager. Hawk. Asks for status every hour. Petty, not evil. The small face of the machine.

## Marsha
- Runs the kitchens on Mars. Mentioned, not seen yet.

## Dr. Lang
- College professor, thick accent, reads off slides. The DC bomb happens in his class.

## Dr. Jiangsu, Dr. Taylor
- Mars. The scientists who first notice the comms problem and ask Alex to check the CommSat.
