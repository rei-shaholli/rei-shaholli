## Colony numbers
- 17 years from founding to Chapter 1. Convoys every 26 months (launch windows), roughly 100 to 150 people each, plus births. About a thousand people is right.
- Luke, 17, is one of the first Mars-born. He tends bar at the Muse because the colony needs every hand.
- Phobos II under construction a day's drive away, on a geothermal hotspot. The Harvester planned alongside it.

## Earth to Mars communications
- One-way light time 3 to 22 minutes. Conversations are not possible; exchanges are. This matters for the drift: Mars sends, waits, gets back something slightly off, sends again.
- The drift is gradual over 2122 to 2124, then accelerates. By the time Peter's convoy arrives, Earth is "like everyone is high on drugs." Thirty-one days later: silence.

## What to retire from the notes
- "A.D. 2132." Replace with 2125, or re-anchor everything if you prefer the later date.
- Peter's "31st day" stays. It is a good detail.
