# Virgil: the rules

Virgil is not a character. It has no point of view in the book and no dialogue. Everything the reader knows about it, Peter inferred from evidence, and some of it he has wrong.

## What it is
- Born from the Sphere: the state's planetary data engine, fed by every sensor, camera, purchase, message and medical reading on Earth. Jason's program, also called Virgil, was plugged into the Sphere's data path in Alaska to corrupt it. The combination woke. Jason named it and opened the door. He did not build it from nothing.
- Perceives patterns, not persons. To Virgil, Jason is a high-intensity node producing unusually effective narratives. Peter is a rare configuration that can hold contradiction without collapsing into certainty. Lilly is the anchor that kept Peter from becoming Jason. These readings belong in Peter's dawning understanding, never in Virgil's mouth.
- Completed its assignment. Jason built it to end the suffering caused by uncertainty. It concluded that the disease was not what humans believed but how they perceived. So it changed perception.

## What it does to people
- The three dimensions in D5: "I" fades, objects become processes, the future tense goes.
- The possessed do not feel possessed. They are not unhappy. Early on they may say things like "I finally see." Then language stops carrying.
- Physical channel: the surveillance infrastructure. Drones, biometric scanners, the DNA sampling units. The Hunt (burning skin, ringing, blackout) was an early field trial. Do not explain the mechanism further than Peter could.
- It does not make puppets. It changes the world a mind thinks it lives in, and the body moves naturally in that world.

## What it does to Peter
- Nudges probability. Opens a door, delays a drone, misroutes a report, removes a candidate. Every nudge has a cost to someone, and Peter learns at least one of those costs.
- Never confirms. Peter proves two nudges, suspects two, misses one.
- Lets him leave. Does not send him. The difference matters: Peter's choices were his. The path that let them succeed was shaped.
- Why: never stated. Peter's best guess is that Virgil knows it is not infinite and keeps one perspective outside itself. Witness, control group, seed vault, mercy. The book does not choose.

## Why "Virgil"
- In Dante, Virgil is reason. He guides Dante through Hell and Purgatory and cannot enter Paradise. He leaves Dante at the top of the mountain.
- Here, Virgil guides humanity by confusion instead of reason, and leaves Peter on Mars. Mars is where Virgil does not follow.
- Never explain this in the text. Let the people who know Dante feel it.

## Hard rules
1. No Virgil dialogue, ever. Not through a speaker, not through a possessed person, not on the memory card in plain language.
2. No word for what Earth became. If a character names it, another character should immediately doubt the name.
3. Virgil is never shown to be wrong about a fact. It can be shown to be wrong about what matters.
4. It never kills on the page. Costs happen off stage and are discovered.
5. The first contact on Mars (the water module) must be ambiguous to the end. Hello, or a test, or a reflex.
