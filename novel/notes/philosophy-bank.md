## 3. Communication is 90% non-verbal
*Theme: logic vs. emotion; society teaches thinking, not feeling.*
*Where: possibly Lilly, who sees Peter through the non-verbal 90%. Or cut.*

Communication is key to societal cohesion.
It is 90% non-verbal and 10% verbal. The verbal portion of communication refers to the information which is communicated and understood from the medium we call language. Language is a product of logic. Logic, of course, is a manifestation of the portion of our being which thinks, analyzes, scrutinises and criticizes, and also the portion which tends to view the world as one big problem, and seeks to solve it. The other 90% is non-verbal, or pertaining to the communication which is understood through non languagental means, such as tone of voice, facial expressions, different gestures, etc; all of which are a product of the portion of our being that is manifested through feelings such as passion, anger, love, etc. In other words, the way we communicate and understand each other is accomplished through a medium other than language, and is more closely related to emotions. And emotions, they sometimes are in conflict with our logical representation of the world... And when they defy our view of the logical world, the way conscience understands it, we are torn apart between understanding and feeling. But yet in this battle, we are taught heed our logical understanding of the world rather than our emotional state within it. And society is happily telling you that that is the way to go. In this society we are taught how to think, and act, but now how to feel. So we are handicapped to the ways we feel. And if we don't know how to feel then we are taught how we should feel.
