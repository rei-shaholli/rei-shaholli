I am the American born son of eastern European parents, Eugenie and Fatima. My parents moved to the USA to escape the third world like conditions of Eastern Europe and to make a better life for themselves. USA, throughout the 20th and the early part of the 21st century, was the economic heart of the world. Due to a mix of physical size and abundant resources, capitalistic, hard working ideologies of the people, the political structure and free markets made for a socio-economic machine unlike any other in recorded history. From its discovery, America was a place where dreams could come true. A place of abundance, of religious/spiritual and political freedom. Take into equation the free market ideology, pioneered by philosophers the likes of John Locke and Adam Smith, and a political structure to govern it, the Democratic, free-market Capitalism was manifested.
