# VIRGIL (working title)

A novel. Started in 2013 at 24. Picked back up in 2026 at 37.

## What this folder is

| Path | What lives here |
|---|---|
| `bible/` | The rules of the book. Thesis, decisions, characters, timeline, how Virgil works. Short files. Read before writing anything. |
| `outline/` | The chapter-by-chapter structure, mapped to Dante's circles. |
| `notes/` | The 2013 material, sorted. `philosophy-bank.md` holds every monologue so it can be quarried. `scene-fragments.md` holds every drafted scene. Nothing is deleted from here. |
| `chapters/draft-v0/` | The original 2013 Chapter 1, for reference only. |
| `chapters/` | The working chapters, one file each, `NN-title.md`. |

## How we work

**Rei owns:** the philosophy, every decision in `bible/01-decisions.md`, taste, and the final word on every line. Rei also writes Peter's reflective passages first, in Rei's own voice, before a chapter is drafted. The 2013 monologues have a real voice in them (the restless rhythm, the ellipses, the way an idea gets circled three times before it lands). Peter is a software engineer, not a writer, telling his story as a legacy. That voice fits him. Do not sand it off.

**Claude drafts:** scenes, dialogue, worldbuilding detail, continuity, the cutting. Claude also keeps the bible honest when a chapter contradicts it.

**Loop per chapter:**
1. Agree on the chapter's job in one sentence (what changes, what the reader learns, which rule of the thesis it demonstrates).
2. Rei writes any reflective passage for it, rough is fine.
3. Claude drafts the chapter around it.
4. Rei reads and marks: `[cut]`, `[wrong, Peter wouldn't]`, `[more]`, `[mine, keep]`.
5. Revise. Move on. Do not polish a chapter until the whole draft exists.

**The one rule:** the book never states its thesis. It demonstrates it. If a paragraph explains the idea instead of showing a consequence of it, it goes to `notes/philosophy-bank.md`, not into the chapter. Exception: Jason may preach, because preaching is his flaw. Peter gets at most one reflective paragraph per chapter.
